One important lesson I have learned from swimming around in the open source world is that a community is not a democracy.  The benefits of a community are for the ones who contribute to the community.  If you don't contribute, you don't get the benefits.  Think about this.  Democracy says everyone is equal and everyone has an equal voice.  Community says only those who contribute to making the community a better place have a voice.  In a community, you can speak all you want, but if you don't contribute no one will listen to you.  They might even tell you to go away.  A community is a meritocracy.

How do you contribute?
