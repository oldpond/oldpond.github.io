What an amazing thing wind power is.  Wind can power a radio.  In fact, it can power satellite communications including GPS communications.  You put up a wind tower with a radio on it, and where the signal starts to drop off you put another one.  You string them all along the main roads.  The revenue from the electricity generated pays for the maintenance of the roads, and you get a new communications network out of it.
