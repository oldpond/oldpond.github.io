The art of speech is a lost art in western societies.  You don't see it taught anymore, at least not in the universities I have attended.

I submit that in the art of speech there can only be two branches: polite conversation and friendly debate.  Polite conversation is carried on for a number of reasons between acquaintences.  I say "friendly debate" because in debate one must challenge the knowledge of one's friends and acquaintences.  This can put a strain on relationships that can have undesireable results.  The debate is "friendly" because when we enter into it we agree that we are exercising our understanding of a subject for the good of all concerned.

At some point in history, debate became a contest.  Red versus blue.  The point of debate became to defeat your opponent and prove your superior intellect.  I believe this was an unfortunate development.  I hope one day the debating societies of the world will wake up and realize they have been heading down the wrong path.  They have created a system designed to divide and conqueor, not enlighten and unify.

Biting wit and cutting satire, again the contest.
