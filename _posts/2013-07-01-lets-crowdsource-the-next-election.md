How would that work?

Well, you would need a site where you could make and accept nominations.

You would need a site where you could crowdfund the expenses associated with running for office.  These would be the exact costs associated with running, not all the optional stuff like advertising, travel expenses, etc.  Just what the government charges to get your name on the ballot.  This would be capped.  Once the costs have been covered, no more donations could be accepted.  This would level the playing field.  If the people like your ideas they will cover the costs of your nomination.  After that, all you need is access to the Internet.  You could run your whole campaign out of your living-room if you wanted.

You would need a site to track platforms.  You would need a site for video speeches.  You could have IRC debates, or video debates, the possibilities are quite intriguing.

And, certain things would have to be restricted to only IP addresses within the borders.  Elections are, after all, very local.

What affect would this have on the creation of a government that would be more in tune with the people?  You could see who people like, who's getting the most karma, that sort of thing.  You could rank the candidates in various ways.  You would have to rank them because there could be hundreds of candidates for each seat.  <strong>Hundreds</strong>.

Think about it.
