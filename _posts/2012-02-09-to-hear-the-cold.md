title: To hear the cold
date: 2012-02-09 23:40

warm
inside
outside
wood soft
snow
the rhythms of snow
slow
graduated
gradual
just finished that
in the warm
in the city
look
to the cold
coursing through
magic magic magic
listen to the cold
the visitor
who comes
who dreams
to give
to take
to hear
to hear the cold coming through
to sing the cold that comes
to sing
